## Overview

The Review Dashboard helps you identify subscriptions that may need attention, using AI analysis to detect:
- **Trials ending soon** - Avoid unwanted charges
- **Unused subscriptions** - Save money on services you're not using
- **Price increases** - Know when costs have gone up
- **Duplicate services** - Eliminate redundant subscriptions

### Key Features
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Analysis** - Automatic detection of subscription issues
- 📊 **Smart Prioritization** - Critical, high, medium, and low priority suggestions
- 💰 **Savings Calculator** - See potential monthly savings
- 🔍 **Advanced Filtering** - Filter by priority and reason
- 📋 **Step-by-Step Cancellation** - AI-generated cancellation instructions

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Dashboard

1. **Login** to your Subscription Tracker account
2. Click **"Reviews"** in the left sidebar navigation
3. The dashboard will automatically load your personalized suggestions

### First Time Setup

The system automatically analyzes your subscriptions. No setup required! However, for best results:
- ✅ Connect your email accounts for activity tracking
- ✅ Keep subscription information up-to-date
- ✅ Check the dashboard weekly for new suggestions

## Review Dashboard

### Dashboard Layout

#### Statistics Cards (Top Section)
The dashboard displays four key metrics:

**📊 Total Suggestions**
- Shows the number of subscriptions needing attention
- Updates automatically when subscriptions change

**🚨 Critical**
- Subscriptions requiring immediate action
- Includes: trials ending today, duplicate charges

**⚠️ High Priority**
- Important actions within the next few days
- Includes: trials ending soon, expensive unused subscriptions

**💰 Potential Savings**
- Estimated monthly savings if you cancel suggested subscriptions
- Automatically normalizes different billing cycles:
  - Yearly subscriptions divided by 12
  - Quarterly subscriptions divided by 3
  - Weekly subscriptions multiplied by 4.33

#### Filter Controls

**Priority Filter**
- **All** - Show all suggestions
- **Critical** - Immediate action required
- **High** - Important within days
- **Medium** - Review recommended
- **Low** - Optional optimization

**Reason Filter**
- **Trial Ending** - Trial ends today
- **Trial Ending Soon** - Trial ends within 3 days
- **Expensive Unused** - High cost + no activity for 30+ days
- **Likely Unused** - No activity for 60+ days
- **Duplicate Detected** - Multiple subscriptions to same provider
- **Price Increased** - Recent price increase detected

**Clear Filters Button**
- Appears when filters are active
- Click to reset all filters to "All"

#### Suggestion Cards

Each suggestion displays:
- **Provider Name** - Service name and icon
- **Amount** - Cost and billing cycle
- **Priority Badge** - Visual indicator (Critical/High/Medium/Low)
- **Reason** - Why this subscription needs attention
- **Message** - Detailed explanation
- **Action Date** - Deadline for action (if applicable)
- **Activity Info** - Days since last email activity
- **Price Change** - Amount of price increase (if applicable)

**Card Actions:**
- Click any card to view full subscription details
- Navigate to cancellation instructions
- Update subscription status

## Understanding Suggestions

### Priority Levels Explained

#### 🔴 Critical Priority
**When Assigned:**
- Trial ends **today**
- Duplicate charges detected
- Immediate financial impact

**Recommended Action:**
- Review and act **immediately**
- Don't wait - trial charges happen automatically

**Example:**
> "Your Netflix trial ends today. Cancel now to avoid being charged $15.99."

#### 🟠 High Priority
**When Assigned:**
- Trial ends within **3 days**
- Expensive subscription (>$20/month) with no activity for 30+ days

**Recommended Action:**
- Review within **24-48 hours**
- Plan cancellation if not needed

**Example:**
> "Your Adobe Creative Cloud trial ends in 2 days. Consider cancelling if you won't use it."

#### 🟡 Medium Priority
**When Assigned:**
- No activity for 60+ days
- Price increased recently
- Moderate cost subscriptions

**Recommended Action:**
- Review within **1 week**
- Evaluate usage vs cost

**Example:**
> "Your Spotify Premium subscription increased from $9.99 to $10.99/month."

#### 🟢 Low Priority
**When Assigned:**
- General optimization opportunities
- Optional reviews

**Recommended Action:**
- Review when convenient
- Consider during monthly budget review

**Example:**
> "Review your Hulu subscription to optimize your streaming costs."

### Reason Codes Explained

#### Trial Ending
**What It Means:**
- Your free trial period ends today
- You'll be charged automatically if you don't cancel

**Why It Matters:**
- Avoid unwanted charges
- Many services auto-renew without reminder

**What To Do:**
1. Evaluate if you want to continue
2. Cancel before trial ends if not needed
3. Set calendar reminder for future trials

#### Trial Ending Soon
**What It Means:**
- Free trial ends within 3 days
- Time to decide before charges begin

**Why It Matters:**
- Gives you time to make informed decision
- Prevents last-minute scramble

**What To Do:**
1. Test the service thoroughly in remaining days
2. Compare with alternatives
3. Cancel if not valuable enough

#### Expensive Unused
**What It Means:**
- High-cost subscription (typically >$20/month)
- No email activity detected for 30+ days
- Likely not using the service

**Why It Matters:**
- Significant monthly savings potential
- Money spent on unused service

**What To Do:**
1. Verify you're truly not using it
2. Consider downgrading instead of cancelling
3. Cancel if confirmed unused

#### Likely Unused
**What It Means:**
- No email activity for 60+ days
- Service appears dormant

**Why It Matters:**
- Even small charges add up
- Better to cancel and re-subscribe if needed

**What To Do:**
1. Try logging in to verify access
2. Check if you use it outside email
3. Cancel if confirmed unused

#### Duplicate Detected
**What It Means:**
- Multiple subscriptions to same provider
- Possible redundant charges

**Why It Matters:**
- Paying twice for same service
- Often happens after account changes

**What To Do:**
1. Verify which subscription is active
2. Cancel the duplicate
3. Consolidate to single account

#### Price Increased
**What It Means:**
- Provider raised subscription price
- Historical pricing data detected change

**Why It Matters:**
- Budget impact
- May exceed value threshold

**What To Do:**
1. Evaluate if still worth the new price
2. Check for grandfathered pricing options
3. Compare with competitors

## Taking Action

### Reviewing a Suggestion

1. **Click the suggestion card** to open subscription details
2. **Review the information:**
   - Current billing amount and cycle
   - Last activity date
   - Service description
   - Payment method
3. **Navigate to Cancellation tab** to see instructions

### Making Decisions

#### Option 1: Keep Subscription
If you decide to keep it:
1. Click **"Mark as Reviewed"** (if implemented)
2. The suggestion will be acknowledged
3. It won't appear in future reviews (unless circumstances change)

#### Option 2: Cancel Subscription
If you decide to cancel:
1. Go to **"Cancellation" tab** in subscription details
2. Click **"Research Cancellation Instructions"**
3. Follow the step-by-step guide provided

#### Option 3: Pause/Downgrade
Some services offer alternatives:
1. Check cancellation instructions for "Pause" option
2. Look for downgrade/tier options
3. Consider seasonal subscriptions (pause summer, resume winter)

## Cancellation Process

### Step-by-Step Cancellation

#### 1. Access Cancellation Instructions

**From Review Dashboard:**
- Click suggestion card → Subscription Detail → Cancellation tab

**Or From Subscriptions List:**
- Navigate to Subscriptions → Select subscription → Cancellation tab

#### 2. Generate AI Instructions

**First Time for Provider:**
1. Click **"Research Cancellation Instructions"** button
2. AI analyzes the provider's cancellation process
3. Wait 10-30 seconds for results (uses OpenAI GPT-4)

**Already Researched:**
- Instructions load automatically
- Cached for 30 days for fast access

#### 3. Review Cancellation Info

The AI provides:

**Cancellation URL**
- Direct link to cancellation page
- Click to open in new tab

**Difficulty Rating**
- 🟢 **Easy** - Self-service online, <5 minutes
- 🟡 **Medium** - Multiple steps or chat, 5-15 minutes
- 🔴 **Hard** - Phone call required, 15+ minutes

**Step-by-Step Instructions**
- Numbered list of exact steps
- Screenshots or descriptions where helpful

**Requirements**
- 📞 Phone call needed? Yes/No
- 💬 Chat required? Yes/No
- ⏸️ Pause option available? Yes/No

**Estimated Time**
- Expected minutes to complete

**Gotchas & Warnings**
- Important things to know
- Common pitfalls to avoid
- Timing considerations

**AI Confidence Score**
- How confident the AI is in the instructions
- Based on source quality and consistency

#### 4. Execute Cancellation

**During Cancellation:**
1. Open the cancellation URL in new tab
2. Follow instructions step-by-step
3. Keep subscription tracker open for reference

**Update Status:**
- **Considering** - Still evaluating
- **Researching** - Reviewing instructions
- **Ready to Cancel** - Have instructions, planning to proceed
- **Cancellation Initiated** - Started cancellation process
- **Awaiting Confirmation** - Waiting for provider confirmation
- **Completed** - Successfully cancelled
- **Failed** - Cancellation attempt failed
- **Abandoned** - Decided not to cancel

**Add Notes:**
- Document any issues encountered
- Note confirmation numbers
- Record cancellation effective date

#### 5. Verify Completion

**After Cancellation:**
1. Update status to **"Completed"**
2. Check for confirmation email
3. Verify no future charges scheduled
4. Download/save any data before access ends

**If Problems Occur:**
1. Update status to **"Failed"**
2. Add notes describing the issue
3. Contact provider support
4. Document resolution steps

## Tips & Best Practices

### Maximizing Savings

**🎯 Act on Critical First**
- Handle trial endings immediately
- Don't let priorities pile up

**📅 Schedule Review Time**
- Check dashboard weekly
- Set calendar reminder

**💡 Use Filters Strategically**
- Start with Critical → High → Medium → Low
- Filter by reason to batch similar actions

**📊 Track Savings**
- Note cancelled subscription costs
- Celebrate your monthly savings

### Avoiding Common Mistakes

**❌ Waiting Too Long**
- Don't wait until trial end day
- Give yourself buffer time

**❌ Ignoring Activity Data**
- Last email date isn't perfect measure
- Verify usage before cancelling

**❌ Cancelling Needed Services**
- Consider if you'll re-subscribe
- Re-subscription can cost more

**❌ Not Documenting**
- Add notes during cancellation
- Save confirmation numbers

### Smart Subscription Management

**🔄 Consider Alternatives**
- Downgrade instead of cancel
- Pause for seasonal services
- Annual vs monthly pricing

**🤝 Share Accounts**
- Family plans save money
- Split costs with friends
- Verify provider policies

**⏰ Set Reminders**
- Calendar alerts before renewals
- Annual subscription reminders
- Trial end dates

**📈 Regular Reviews**
- Monthly budget review
- Quarterly subscription audit
- Annual cost analysis

### When to Keep Subscriptions

**Don't Cancel If:**
- ✅ Use it regularly (weekly or more)
- ✅ Cost justified by value received
- ✅ No good alternatives available
- ✅ Locked-in promotional pricing
- ✅ Long-term contracts with penalties

**Consider Keeping:**
- Annual subscriptions mid-year (unless refund available)
- Services with stored data you need
- Subscriptions with high re-signup fees
- Services used seasonally (but pause if available)

## Troubleshooting

### Dashboard Not Loading

**Symptoms:**
- Spinner shows indefinitely
- Error message appears

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Check internet connection
3. Verify you're logged in
4. Clear browser cache
5. Try different browser

### Suggestions Not Appearing

**Symptoms:**
- Dashboard shows "All good! 🎉" but you have subscriptions

**Possible Reasons:**
- All subscriptions are healthy
- Email tracking not connected
- Recent subscriptions (< 7 days)
- Trial dates not set

**Solutions:**
1. Connect email accounts for activity tracking
2. Update subscription details (trial end dates, etc.)
3. Wait for next analysis cycle (runs nightly)

### Cancellation Instructions Missing

**Symptoms:**
- "Research Cancellation Instructions" button does nothing
- Error message appears

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Try again in a few minutes (API rate limits)
3. Verify provider name is correct
4. Use "Force Refresh" option if available

### Wrong Priority or Reason

**Symptoms:**
- Suggestion doesn't seem right
- Priority seems off

**Explanation:**
- Algorithm uses: trial dates, activity, pricing, duplicates
- Last email activity may not reflect actual usage
- You may use service without generating emails

**Solutions:**
1. Review suggestion anyway
2. Mark as reviewed to dismiss
3. Update subscription details if incorrect
4. Ignore low-accuracy suggestions

## Privacy & Security

### Data Usage

**What We Track:**
- Subscription details you enter
- Email activity timestamps (not content)
- Cancellation status updates
- Provider research results (cached globally)

**What We Don't Track:**
- Email message content
- Payment credentials
- Personal communications
- Cancellation notes (stored privately)

### AI & OpenAI

**Cancellation Research:**
- Uses OpenAI GPT-4 for provider research
- Only provider name sent to OpenAI
- Results cached for 30 days
- No personal data included in AI requests

**Your Privacy:**
- AI never sees your email content
- Only analyzes: provider name, your activity timestamps
- Cancellation notes are private to your account

## Glossary

**Activity Tracking** - Monitoring email timestamps to detect service usage

**Billing Cycle** - How often you're charged (weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly)

**Critical Priority** - Requires immediate action today

**Force Refresh** - Request new AI research, bypassing cache

**Priority** - Urgency level (Critical, High, Medium, Low)

**Provider** - The company/service you're subscribed to

**Reason Code** - Category explaining why subscription needs review

**Suggestion** - AI-generated recommendation to review a subscription

**Trial Period** - Free temporary access before paid subscription begins
